Teaching language models to improve language models autonomously. 让语言模型自主改进语言模型。

Zhaojian Yu · Penghao Yin · Shuzheng Gao · Shilin He · Kai Cai · Xiao-Ping Zhang

Autonomous post-training is more than coding 自主后训练不只是写代码

Language-model agents can write software, yet improving a model requires a longer operational loop: planning an iteration, constructing benchmark-aligned data, running stable training, evaluating checkpoints, and preserving evidence across many hours. 语言模型 Agent 已经能够编写软件，但改进模型需要一条更长的操作闭环：规划迭代、构建与评测目标一致的数据、稳定训练、评估检查点，并在持续数小时的过程中保存证据与实验状态。

AutoTrainess turns that loop into a training-specialized Agent–Computer Interface called AutoTrainHub. It externalizes reusable workflows, rules, and constraints so an agent can act on clearer interfaces instead of an underspecified raw command line. AutoTrainess 将这条闭环构建为面向训练的 Agent–Computer Interface——AutoTrainHub。它把可复用的工作流、规则与约束显式化，让 Agent 不必只依赖定义不足的原始命令行环境。

Results on PostTrainBench PostTrainBench 实验结果

Under the same 10-hour H20 GPU budget, the paper reports higher average scores for AutoTrainess than for CLI-only agents across multiple model and agent-harness combinations. 在相同的 10 小时 H20 GPU 预算下，论文报告显示：在多种模型与 Agent Harness 组合中，AutoTrainess 的平均得分均高于 CLI-only Agent。

A concrete step toward AI4AI 迈向 AI4AI 的具体一步

AutoTrainess studies a practical form of AI4AI: AI systems operating the workflows that build and improve other AI systems. Its interfaces also provide a testbed for self-improving agents and self-improving skills grounded in measurable training and evaluation evidence. AutoTrainess 探索一种具体的 AI4AI：由 AI 系统运行构建和改进其他 AI 系统的工作流。它也为自改进 Agent 与自改进技能提供了一个以训练和评测证据为基础的实验平台。
